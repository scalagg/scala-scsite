In order to use any part of the Solara Service you must agree to both our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.


Our Solara Minecraft Server
When you connect to our Java Edition server, we collect certain data in order to provide you with the best possible experience. This data includes, but is not limited to:

  • Your Minecraft Account UUID, name, last login, and rank
  • Your currency data
  • Player statistics
  • Purchases
  • Cosmetics and related choices
  • Your daily reward streaks
  • Monthly reward streaks
  • Your list of friends and your friend requests
  • Guild membership
  • Your logins (ip, client version)
  • Any and all poll responses including answers
  • What players you have ignored
  • Your user preferences
  • ELO and ranked timeouts
  • Kit data
  • Game and map voting
  • Advertisement participation

When you interact with our website and forums, we collect certain data in order to provide you with the best possible experience. This data includes, but is not limited to:
